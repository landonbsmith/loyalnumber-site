How it works

  1. Set up your line. You sign up, confirm your email with a one-time code, and agree to our text-message terms. You get a US phone number for your agent.
  2. We test it. Before you rely on it, we check that the line can call and receive texts, so you know it really works.
  3. Your agent gets to work. Ask your agent to make a call, check a text, or grab a login code. It uses your LoyalNumber line to do it.

Honest on every call

Every call from a LoyalNumber line starts by saying it's an AI assistant calling on your behalf, and that the call is recorded. This can't be turned off. LoyalNumber is for calls and texts about your own business and errands. No robocalls, no cold calling, no spam, and no bulk or marketing texts.
