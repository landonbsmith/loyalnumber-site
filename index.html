What it does

Made for regular people and small businesses.

You don't need to be a programmer. If you use an AI assistant to help with errands, calls, or your business, LoyalNumber gives it a phone number that belongs to you, so your personal number stays private.

  • Calls

    Your agent can call a business for you, like your internet company or a doctor's office, and you get a transcript afterward.

  • Texts

    Your agent can read texts sent to your number and send texts only when you tell it to.

  • Login codes

    Your agent can receive the login and verification codes that services text to your number. Codes are deleted automatically after 24 hours.

  • Straight answers

    A text shows as delivered only when the phone carrier confirms it. If something fails, you see that it failed.

How it works

Three steps, then it just works.

  1. Set up your line.

    You sign up, confirm your email with a one-time code, and agree to our text-message terms. You get a US phone number for your agent.

  2. We test it.

    Before you rely on it, we check that the line can call and receive texts, so you know it really works.

  3. Your agent gets to work.

    Ask your agent to make a call, check a text, or grab a login code. It uses your LoyalNumber line to do it.

Honest on every call

“Hi, I'm an AI assistant calling for Jordan. This call is recorded.”

Every call from a LoyalNumber line starts by saying it's an AI assistant calling on your behalf, and that the call is recorded. This can't be turned off. LoyalNumber is for calls and texts about your own business and errands. No robocalls, no cold calling, no spam, and no bulk or marketing texts.
